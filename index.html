    Research case study

    Operational Challenges in the Aviation Industry: A Case of Air Zimbabwe

    Honours dissertation, University of Zimbabwe. Qualitative research.

    44years studied, 1980–2024

    The problem

    National airlines operate in a competitive and changing global industry, and their difficulties rarely have a single cause. I set out to explain the long-term factors behind Air Zimbabwe’s performance and decline, and what could be done about them.

    How I approached it

    I traced the airline’s history from 1980 to 2024, analysed information from relevant research sources and examined its institutional weaknesses through four lenses.

    • Historical legaciesWhat did the airline inherit, and how did it shape later performance?
    • Governance gapsWhere did the institution’s structures and oversight need strengthening?
    • Political influencesHow did politics affect the way the airline operated?
    • Economic pressuresWhich economic conditions constrained its performance?

    What I recommended

    The dissertation closes with practical recommendations in three areas.

    • Governance improvementsTo address the institutional weaknesses identified.
    • Strategic planningFor a competitive and evolving sector.
    • Fleet renewalAs a route to operational improvement.

    What this shows an employer

    • I can manage a long, independent research project through to a finished piece of work.
    • I can look past symptoms and explain how history, politics, money and management interact inside an organisation.
    • I can move from analysis to practical recommendations, the same step needed in reports, policy briefs and programme reviews.
